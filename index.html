Privacy Policy

Light Cine

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Overview

Light Cine respects your privacy. This app is a cinema-first exposure and lighting assistant. Camera frames and lighting analysis are processed entirely on your device.

1. Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app does not require user accounts or authentication.

2. Camera and Photo Library Access

The app may request access to the following:

  • Camera – for live cinema exposure metering, scopes, subject region detection, and lighting analysis.
  • Photo Library – only if you choose to attach an optional reference still to a saved shot.

All access is initiated by the user and used only for the app’s core functionality. Camera frames are processed on-device and are never uploaded.

3. On-Device Processing

Metering, false color, histogram, waveform, zebras, subject region tools, lighting ratio, and project notes are handled locally on your device. Optional projects you create are stored only on your device. No camera frames or project data are uploaded to any server.

4. Face / Subject Tools

When subject mode is enabled, the app may use on-device Vision APIs to detect face regions for exposure guidance. This is region detection only. The app does not perform identity recognition and does not upload face data.

5. Third-Party Services

Light Cine does not use third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, or cloud services.

6. Children’s Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no data is collected, no personal information is stored for any user.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future updates introduce features that affect privacy, this policy will be updated accordingly and reflected in the App Store privacy disclosures.
